Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · ASCENT APP, LLC

Who we are

Ourise is a habit-tracking app run by ASCENT APP, LLC, a Florida limited liability company (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. Questions: ourisesupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, name, username, and profile photo if you add one.
  • What you create: habits, goals, completions, points and streaks, your routine, calendar events, journal entries and mood, messages, group chats, group goals, and reports you send.
  • Onboarding answers: what you tell us when you set up the app (your focus, what gets in your way, what you want to achieve).
  • Crash information: when something breaks, an error report with your device type and an anonymous account number, never your name or email. We do not record your screen or track which features you use.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To run the app: save your progress, show it to you, and share what you choose with your friends.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, remove content, and ban accounts that break our Terms.
  • To fix problems (crash information).
  • To provide AI features, only after you agree (see below).

We never sell your data, never show ads, and never track you across other apps or websites.

Who can see your information

  • Your friends (people whose friend request was accepted) see your name, username, photo, points and streak, and only the habits you set as public or shared with them. Private habits are never shown to anyone else.
  • Members of a group goal you join see your progress on that goal’s habits, following each habit’s own privacy setting.
  • Messages are seen only by the people in that conversation.
  • Your journal, calendar, routine, and weekly overview are yours alone.
  • If someone blocks you, or you block them, neither of you can see the other.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Base44: hosting, database, sign-in, and file storage.
  • Anthropic (through Base44): powers the AI features, only after you agree to them.
  • Sentry: crash reports, sent automatically when something breaks, without your name or email.
  • Apple and Google: if you choose to sign in with them.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

AI features

Ourise can suggest habits and write a weekly overview of your progress. To do this, the relevant information (for example your onboarding answers, or your habits, goals, and completions for the week) is sent to Anthropic’s AI through Base44. This happens only after you agree, and you can turn AI features off in Settings. Your journal and messages are not sent to AI. AI suggestions can be wrong; use your own judgment.

Sensitive information

We treat your mood, journal entries, and onboarding answers as sensitive, health-related information. We use them only to provide the features you use, never sell them, never use them for advertising, and send them to AI only with your consent.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account closes immediately, and your data (habits, goals, journal, messages, and the rest) is then removed from our systems. Photos you uploaded may remain with our storage provider for a time; email us to ask for their removal.

We may keep limited records where needed for safety or the law, for example a record of a ban, or a report about abuse.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with access limited to you and the people you choose. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Age

Ourise is for people 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Where Ourise is available

Ourise is currently offered in the United States. Your information is stored and processed in the United States.

Your choices

  • See and edit most of your information in the app at any time.
  • Choose who sees each habit, block people, and mute notifications.
  • Turn AI feedback on or off in Settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by emailing ourisesupport@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
