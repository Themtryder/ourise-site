Support

Inpact · ASCENT APP, LLC

Contact us

Email ourisesupport@gmail.com. We read every message and reply as soon as we can, usually within a day or two. From the app: Settings → Contact support fills in your account for you.

Delete your account

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account closes immediately and your data (habits, goals, journal, messages and the rest) is then removed. Photos you uploaded may remain with our storage provider for a time; email ourisesupport@gmail.com to ask for their removal.

Report or block someone

Open their profile or the conversation and choose Report or Block. We review reports within 24 hours.

Your data

To ask for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion, email ourisesupport@gmail.com. How we handle your information: Privacy Policy.
